UCC Cargo Inspection

Our UCC cargo inspection services involve physical inspection of the available cargo and documentation of relevant observations according to the scope of the assignment.

The inspection may consider:
Nature and description of cargo
Physical condition
Quantity or visible characteristics
Packaging and identification
Visible damage
General condition
Photographic records
Other relevant technical observations
