Preparation Checklist

Documents Required for Engineering Inspection and Valuation

To expedite on-site verification for Chennai customs clearance, EPCG nexus verification, or factory stability certification, clients typically provide:

  • Company details and contact information
  • Machinery or equipment specifications
  • Purchase invoice or import documentation, where applicable
  • Equipment make, model and serial number
  • Manufacturing year and machinery age
  • Photographs and equipment details
  • Previous valuation or inspection reports, if available
  • Relevant Customs, DGFT or other authority documents, where applicable
  • Details of the intended certification or inspection purpose

Our team can clarify the document requirements after reviewing the specific assignment.
